## ⚠️ Read first
- **Mali, Burkina Faso & Niger left ECOWAS on 29 Jan 2025.** ECOWAS asked members to keep free movement "until further notice", but most governments advise **against travel** there.
- The "~67 destinations" figure (Henley 2026: 66th–68th) includes more places than this list of 41 (e.g. Haiti, Micronesia, BVI, Montserrat).
- Several Caribbean/Asia routings can need **transit visas** for Ghanaians (Schengen airport-transit, UK DATV, US transit). Check every connection.
- Singapore, Kenya, Tanzania, Uganda, Zimbabwe, Zambia entry details vary by source — verify with the official portal.

## ECOWAS (West Africa)

### 🇧🇯 Benin
**Entry:** Visa-free · 90 days — ECOWAS free movement; carry passport + yellow fever card.

| | |
|---|---|
| ✈️ Flight (return) | $170–$320 · ~1h45 or 5–6h by road via Togo |
| 🏨 Hotel / night (budget · mid · luxury) | $25 · $60 · $150 |
| 🏠 Airbnb / night | ~$45 |
| 🍽️ Daily spend (budget · mid · comfort) | $20 · $40 · $90 |
| 📅 Best time | Nov–Mar |
| 🛡️ Safety | 🟡 Moderate — Southern Benin is calm; the far north (Pendjari/W parks near Burkina) has advisories. |
| 💱 Currency / language | XOF (CFA franc) · French, Fon |

**Getting there:** Direct/1-stop flights to Cotonou; most overland via Aflao–Lomé–Hillacondji.

**Best spots**
- Ouidah — Route des Esclaves, Door of No Return, Python Temple
- Ganvié — stilt village on Lake Nokoué
- Abomey — UNESCO royal palaces
- Grand-Popo — quiet palm-lined beach & lagoon
- Dantokpa Market, Cotonou — one of West Africa's biggest

**Best activities**
- Attend Vodun Days in Ouidah (10 January)
- Pirogue ride through Ganvié
- Moto-taxi (zémidjan) around Cotonou
- Safari in Pendjari NP (only if advisories allow)

**Eat**
- Pâte rouge with fish
- Akassa & sauce
- Poulet bicyclette

**Events:** Vodun Days (Jan 10), Gèlèdè festivals

**Tips**
- Zémidjans are cheap — agree price first
- Bring cash (XOF); cards rare outside hotels
- Combine with Togo for a 2-country trip

**What's not great**
- Northern border zones
- Rainy peaks Jun–Jul make roads muddy
- Limited nightlife infrastructure

**Eat**

- Grogue & ponche

**Tips**

- Euro is widely accepted in resorts
- Visit outside Aug–Oct heat

**What's not great**
- Dust haze in Harmattan months
- Island-hopping logistics
- Prices high in Sal resorts
