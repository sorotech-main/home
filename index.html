41 destinations open to Ghanaian passport holders without a prior visa: ECOWAS free movement, visa-on-arrival and online-authorisation countries. Each one has flights from Accra, best months, hotel and Airbnb costs, safety, a Black Traveller Index, photos of the place, things to do and TikTok/YouTube searches. Tap "Click to view more" on any country to open it. Filter it down, shortlist what you like and hide what you don't.

Check before you book. Mali, Burkina Faso and Niger left ECOWAS in January 2025 and most governments advise against travel there. The "about 67 destinations" figure counts more places than this list. Several Caribbean and Asia routings can need transit visas for Ghanaians, so check every connection.
